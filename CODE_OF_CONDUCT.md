# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We as contributors and maintainers pledge to make participation in this project a respectful experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include being kind and patient, welcoming different viewpoints, accepting constructive feedback, and focusing on what is best for the community.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include harassment, insults or personal attacks, public or private abuse, publishing others' private information without permission, and other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Responsibilities and enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards and may remove, edit, or reject contributions that do not follow this Code. Maintainers will communicate reasons for moderation decisions when practical.

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the repository maintainers. Maintainers will review reports and take action appropriate to the circumstances.

This Code of Conduct is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org), version 2.1.
